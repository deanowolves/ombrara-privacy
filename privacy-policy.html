OMBRARA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Who we are

OMBRARA is a manual money-management app provided by Dean Bloomfield. For privacy questions or support, contact bloomfield.dean@sky.com. OMBRARA is not a bank and does not connect to bank accounts or move money.

Information stored by the app

The app stores the accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring schedules and preferences you enter on your device. It uses these records to calculate balances, forecasts, insights and budget suggestions. It also stores reminder and backup settings. No OMBRARA online account is required.

Collection and sharing

This version does not send your financial records to the developer or an OMBRARA server. It has no advertising or analytics integration. We do not sell your information. Device operating systems and services you choose to use have their own privacy practices.

Backups and device transfers

Manual backups contain your financial records as unencrypted JSON files. You choose where to save them. Choosing a cloud-storage provider may upload the file to that provider under its own terms. Keep backups private and do not send them for support unless specifically needed and you choose to do so. Separately, your operating system may include app data in its device backup or device-transfer features, depending on your settings. OMBRARA does not operate that service or receive those backups.

Notifications and permissions

Optional local notifications remind you about budget reviews. Permission can be refused or disabled in device settings. The app uses the device time zone to schedule reminders and can restore reminders after a device restart. Import and export use the system file picker to access files you select.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them or clear the app data. Some delete actions deliberately preserve financial history; they are not a complete data-erasure function. On Android, clear OMBRARA storage in device settings to erase the local ledger. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but operating-system backups may restore it on reinstall. Delete exported files and manage device or cloud backups separately. We cannot remotely access or delete your local ledger.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you provide. We use them to answer your enquiry and investigate issues, through our email provider. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for support and any applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us. Please avoid sending sensitive financial information unnecessarily.

Children

OMBRARA is intended for a general adult audience and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through support.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current version without live purchases or advertising. If data practices change, we will update this policy and any required disclosures before introducing those changes.
